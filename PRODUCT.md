# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Primary users are admin and tutor staff who operate the LMS every day. They need to build and publish courses, manage student cohorts, watch renewals, handle finance, and keep the learning operation moving without friction.

Students are the secondary audience on the same product surface. Their experience should feel motivating, clear, and easy to resume, but the product needs to serve the admin/tutor workflow first.

## Product Purpose

This product is a multi-tenant LMS for online chemistry education that supports SaaS delivery and self-hosted licensing. The main job is to help tutors run a course business, publish structured lessons, track students, and support learning with AI-assisted study features.

Success means the admin and tutor team can manage content and students quickly, and students can return to learning with minimal friction.

## Brand Personality

Energetic, trustworthy, sharp.

The interface should feel active and motivating, but not noisy. It should suggest progress, movement, and momentum while still reading as a serious professional tool.

## Anti-references

Avoid generic SaaS gray dashboards, flat admin panels with no hierarchy, and loud gaming-style visuals. Avoid sterile corporate UI that feels like a spreadsheet wrapped in cards. Avoid overdecorated “AI startup” surfaces with random gradients and vague copy.

## Design Principles

- Make the admin workflow the fastest path on the page, because that is the main operating surface.
- Keep hierarchy obvious so course structure, student status, and finance signals are readable at a glance.
- Make learning feel active and encouraging, so students want to continue from where they left off.
- Use motion and emphasis purposefully, not everywhere at once.
- Keep the experience premium but legible, with enough contrast and spacing for long working sessions.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Design for strong readability, keyboard support, and responsive behavior across desktop, tablet, and mobile. Preserve sufficient contrast for body text and important status indicators. Minimize motion where users prefer reduced animation, and keep interaction targets large enough for touch devices.
